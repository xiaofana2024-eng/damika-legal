Damika Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Damika is an on-device tea inventory and brew archive app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles on your device.

Information stored on your device

  • Tea cards, brew recipes, drafts, restock marks, and brew credit balances
  • Photos you capture or select for tea archives, stored in the app sandbox
  • Purchase transaction identifiers needed for StoreKit consumable bookkeeping

Damika does not operate an account system and does not upload your tea archive to Damika servers.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you take photos of tea cakes, dry leaf, or liquor color.
  • Photo Library: used only when you choose or save photos for brew cards.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: used only when you start voice capture for brew parameters.
  • App Tracking Transparency: the system prompt may appear once on first home. This build does not use IDFA for advertising after the prompt.
  • Notifications: the system prompt may appear once on first home. This build does not send local or remote notifications after the prompt.

Purchases

Consumable brew packs are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple provides transaction receipts; Damika stores verification results and granted credits locally for idempotency.

Data retention and deletion

Use Clear All Data in Settings to remove teas, recipes, drafts, media files, purchase history, and remaining credits from this device.

Contact

This policy accompanies the Damika demo distribution. For device permission issues, use iOS Settings.
